About the Project

Climate Intelligence Project Overview

This website presents a student-friendly prototype for a digital climate system that uses monitored data to identify heatwave conditions, predict risks, and issue early advisories.

Project Objectives

  • Understand how climate data can support heatwave monitoring.
  • Design a simple dashboard for real-time risk visualization.
  • Show how prediction models can guide public safety planning.
  • Explain the importance of early warning systems in disaster preparedness.

What is Climate Intelligence?

Climate intelligence refers to the use of weather observations, historical records, and analytical tools to understand environmental risks and support better decision-making.

Workflow

1
Data Collection

Gather temperature, humidity, and climate observations.

2
Monitoring

Track daily conditions and identify unusual patterns.

3
Prediction

Estimate likely heatwave intensity and timeframe.

4
Risk Assessment

Classify risk into low, moderate, high, or extreme.

5
Early Warning

Inform communities about safe actions and preparedness.

Possible Data Sources

  • Weather stations and meteorological agencies
  • Satellite and remote sensing data
  • Open climate repositories and public datasets
  • Historical temperature and humidity records

Technologies Used

  • HTML5 for page structure
  • CSS3 for responsive modern styling
  • JavaScript for dynamic UI and charts
  • Bootstrap 5 and Chart.js for dashboard presentation
